01. The Problem: The Cognitive Penalty of DeFi

Existing non-custodial Web3 wallets suffer from severe usability friction: 42-character hexadecimal addresses, ambiguous slippage tolerance parameters, and disjointed multi-chain balances that cause over 70% of new users to abandon transactions out of error paralysis.

My objective with Elektra was to engineer a neobank-grade consumer interface that preserves self-custody principles while replacing technical anxiety with intuitive, guided intent recognition.

02. Conversational Intent Architecture (Voice-to-Chain)

Rather than forcing users through multi-step dropdown menus, token address lookups, and manual gas sliders, Elektra introduces a dedicated natural-language voice interface.

A single voice prompt—such as "Transfer 50 USDT to John"—is parsed instantly into recipient ENS resolution, network auto-detection (ERC-20), and gas fee estimation, culminating in an unambiguous single-tap confirmation sheet.

3-Phase Verification Safeguard:

  • Phase 1: Real-Time Acoustic Feedback with animated audio visualizer ring.
  • Phase 2: Intent Validation resolving address books and smart contract safety score.
  • Phase 3: Explicit Modal Authorization detailing network, gas, and final recipient.

03. Transparent Swaps & Institutional Telemetry

The Swap engine surfaces transparent routing metrics directly on the input card: Uniswap v3 (Base) routing, expected network fee ($3.08), maximum price impact (0.15%), and slippage tolerance (0.50%). Users always understand their exact execution price before signing.

The asset telemetry view features interactive candlestick and line charts across 1H, 1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, and All timeframes, coupled with portfolio weight percentage gauges and 24-hour delta indicators.
