THE PHILOSOPHY

Data without design is sterile. Design without data is decoration.

Most digital products suffer from one of two extremes: they are either visually immaculate but economically dysfunctional, or packed with features yet agonizing to navigate.

My academic background in Quantitative Economics fundamentally shapes how I approach product strategy. Before opening Figma or writing component architectures, I dissect user incentives, decision fatigue, and conversion mechanics. Every typography hierarchy, button layout, and micro-interaction is evaluated through cognitive load and measurable business impact.

Over the past 5+ years, I’ve applied this dual perspective across consumer commerce platforms, multi-market logistics telemetry, and venture-scale identities—helping startups unlock over $2M in seed capital and engineering frictionless mobile ordering flows that reduced drop-off by 35%.
