ENGAGEMENT & PROCESS

How we partner, execute, and deliver results.

Most design starts and ends with surface aesthetics. Because of my background in quantitative economics, every layout decision, button placement, and onboarding sequence is evaluated through cognitive load, friction points, and conversion metrics. You get products that look world-class and deliver measurable business outcomes.

I partner primarily with early-stage to Series-A founders who need an MVP designed from scratch, a venture-winning pitch deck, or a comprehensive redesign to scale. I also consult for growth teams looking to overhaul complex internal tools or dashboards.

Sprint cycles typically run between 3 to 6 weeks depending on product scope. We establish clear milestone deliverables from week one, working in tight weekly syncs with interactive Figma prototypes and production-ready handoffs.

Both. I work in Figma and Framer for rapid iteration and design systems, and can build or collaborate directly with your engineering team in React, Next.js, Tailwind, and Webflow to ensure the shipped product matches the pixel-perfect design.

I operate on structured fixed-fee project scopes or monthly dedicated sprint retainers. No ambiguous hourly billing—every deliverable, timeline, and outcome is clearly defined upfront.
